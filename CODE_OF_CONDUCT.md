# Code of Conduct

This project adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/)
as its code of conduct.

In short: be respectful and constructive, assume good faith, and focus on the work. Harassment,
personal attacks and discriminatory language are not acceptable in any project space (issues, pull
requests, discussions or any other channel).

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behaviour to the project maintainers privately. Use GitHub's
"Report content" feature, or contact a maintainer listed in the repository. Reports are handled
confidentially. Maintainers who do not follow or enforce the Code of Conduct may face consequences
decided by the other maintainers.

Enforcement follows the
[Contributor Covenant enforcement guidelines](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/#enforcement-guidelines).
